How to Convert Text to Speech Online

This tool reads your text aloud using the voices that are already installed in your browser and device. Nothing is sent to a server, and there is nothing to install.

  1. Paste or type your text: Click the text box and add the words you want to hear. Long texts are fine, they are read in parts.
  2. Choose a language and voice: The list shows every voice available on your device. Pick the language first, then the voice you like.
  3. Adjust speed, pitch and volume: Slow the speed down for learning or proofreading, or speed it up to listen faster.
  4. Press Speak: Use Pause and Resume if you need a break, or Stop to start again from the beginning.

Key Features

  • Many languages and voices: Uses the voices on your own device, so the choice depends on your system and browser. Chrome, Edge and Safari usually offer the most.
  • Speed, pitch and volume controls: Fine-tune how the text sounds, from slow and clear to fast listening.
  • Long text support: Text is split at sentence ends so long articles are read without stopping halfway.
  • Private by design: The text is read on your device. It is not uploaded or stored.
  • Free with no limits: No account, no character limit and no watermark.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does it support Urdu, Hindi and Arabic?

Yes, if your device has a voice for that language. Android phones and Chrome usually include Urdu, Hindi and Arabic voices. On Windows you can add voices in Settings, Time and Language, Speech. If no voice is found, the tool tells you.

Why are there different voices on different devices?

The tool uses the speech voices built into your browser and operating system, so the list changes from device to device. This is also why nothing needs to be uploaded.

Can I download the speech as an MP3 file?

No. Browsers do not allow web pages to record their own speech output, so this tool only plays the audio. If you need an audio file you would have to record your device output with a separate recorder.

Why does the speech stop on long texts in other tools?

Some browsers stop reading after about 15 seconds. This tool avoids that by splitting your text into short parts and reading them one after another.

Is my text uploaded or saved?

No. The text is spoken by your own browser and device. Fixlytools does not receive or store it.

Why is there no sound?

Check that the volume slider and your device volume are up, that the device is not on silent, and that you have chosen a voice. Press Speak again after changing the voice.
