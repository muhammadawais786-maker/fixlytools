How to Use the Online Stopwatch and Timer

Use the stopwatch to time something that is already happening, and the countdown timer to be alerted after a set time. Both keep correct time even if you switch to another tab.

  1. Stopwatch: press Start: The time runs in hours, minutes, seconds and hundredths of a second. Press Pause to stop it and Resume to continue.
  2. Record laps: Press Lap while it is running to save a split time. The table shows each lap and the running total, and marks the fastest and slowest laps.
  3. Timer: set the time: Open the Countdown Timer tab, type hours, minutes and seconds or tap a preset like 5 minutes, then press Start.
  4. Wait for the alarm: When the timer reaches zero it plays a sound and vibrates a phone that supports it. Press Stop alarm to silence it.

Key Features

  • Hundredth-of-a-second precision: The stopwatch is accurate and does not drift when the tab is in the background.
  • Lap times: Record unlimited laps with split and total time, and copy them.
  • Countdown timer with alarm: Set any time up to 99 hours, with a progress bar and a sound when it ends.
  • Quick presets: One tap for 1, 5, 10, 15, 25, 30 and 60 minutes. 25 minutes suits the Pomodoro study method.
  • Time in the tab title: The remaining time shows in the browser tab, so you can see it while working elsewhere.
  • Free and private: No sign-up and nothing is uploaded.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is the stopwatch accurate?

Yes. It measures real clock time, not the number of ticks, so it stays correct even when the browser slows down background tabs. The display updates about 30 times a second.

Will the timer still work if I switch tabs or lock my phone?

The countdown keeps the correct end time, so it shows the right time when you return. However, some phones pause web pages when the screen is locked, so the alarm sound may be delayed. For important alarms, keep the screen on or use your phone clock app.

Why did I not hear the alarm sound?

Make sure the sound option is ticked and your device volume is up. Browsers only play sound after you have interacted with the page, which pressing Start counts as.

How do I use the Pomodoro technique?

Choose the 25 min preset, work until the alarm rings, then take a 5 minute break by choosing 5 min. After four rounds, take a longer break.

How do I use laps?

While the stopwatch runs, press Lap each time you complete a round. The table shows each lap time and the total so far, for example for running or swimming practice.

What is the maximum time for the timer?

You can set up to 99 hours, 59 minutes and 59 seconds.
