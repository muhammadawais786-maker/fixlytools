Frequently Asked Questions

How do I calculate a percentage of a number?

Multiply the number by the percentage and divide by 100. For example 15% of 200 is 200 x 15 / 100 = 30.

How do I calculate percentage increase?

Subtract the old value from the new value, divide by the old value and multiply by 100. Going from 50 to 65 is (65 - 50) / 50 x 100 = 30% increase.

How do I work out a discount?

Use "Add or subtract a percentage", choose Decrease, enter the original price and the discount percent. A 20% discount on 1000 gives a sale price of 800.

How do I add tax or VAT to a price?

Choose Increase, enter the price and the tax rate. Adding 17% to 1000 gives 1170.

Why is a 50% increase followed by a 50% decrease not back to the start?

The second percentage is taken from a larger number. 100 up 50% is 150, and 150 down 50% is 75, not 100.

What is the difference between percentage points and percent?

Going from 10% to 12% is a rise of 2 percentage points, but it is a 20% increase in the rate itself. This tool's percentage change box calculates the 20% kind.
