How to Convert Units Online

Pick a category, type a value and choose the units. The answer updates as you type, and the table below shows the same value in every other unit of that category.

  1. Choose a category: Select Length, Weight, Temperature, Area, Volume, Speed, Time, Data, Pressure or Energy.
  2. Enter a value: Type the number you want to convert. Decimals and negative numbers (for temperature) work too.
  3. Pick the units: Choose the unit you have in the From box and the unit you need in the To box. Use Swap units to reverse them.
  4. Read the result: The result appears instantly. The All Conversions table shows the value in every unit of the category at once.

Frequently Asked Questions

How do I convert Celsius to Fahrenheit?

Multiply the Celsius value by 9/5 and add 32. For example 100 degrees C is 212 degrees F. The tool does this for you in the Temperature category, and also converts to Kelvin.

How many square feet are in a marla?

This tool uses the common definition of 1 marla = 272.25 square feet (about 25.29 square meters), and 1 kanal = 20 marla. Marla sizes can differ by region and by local land records, so check the definition used in your area before buying or selling property.

What is the difference between KB and KiB?

KB (kilobyte) is 1000 bytes and is used by storage makers. KiB (kibibyte) is 1024 bytes and is used by many operating systems. The tool offers both so you can pick the one you need.

How much is a tola in grams?

One tola is 11.6638038 grams, which is the standard used for gold in Pakistan and India.

Are US and UK gallons different?

Yes. A US gallon is 3.785 liters and a UK (imperial) gallon is 4.546 liters. Both are available in the Volume category.

Is the converter accurate enough for science or engineering?

It uses exact conversion factors and shows up to 10 significant digits. For critical work always double check against the standard your project requires.
