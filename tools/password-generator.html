Best Practices for Password Security

  • Use at least 16 characters: Longer passwords take billions of years to crack with brute-force dictionary attacks.
  • Never reuse passwords: If one website suffers a leak, unique passwords ensure your other accounts remain protected.
  • Client-Side Cryptography: Fixlytools uses crypto.getRandomValues() for entropy, keeping your passwords 100% private.
