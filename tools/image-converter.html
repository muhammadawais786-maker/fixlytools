Why Convert Images to WebP, JPG, or PNG?

Choosing the right image format is essential for web performance and compatibility:

  • WebP: Next-gen format developed by Google offering superior 30% higher compression with transparency support.
  • PNG: Lossless format ideal for graphics, logos, illustrations, and transparent backgrounds.
  • JPG: Universal standard supported by every email client, printer, and device on Earth.
