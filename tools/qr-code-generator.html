How to Create a QR Code Online

QR codes are now used everywhere — on business cards, restaurant menus, product packaging, social media profiles, and payment pages. Fixlytools QR Code Generator lets you create QR codes for any URL, plain text, or Wi-Fi credentials in seconds — no account needed.

  1. Select QR Content Type: Choose what you want the QR code to contain: a website URL, plain text, or Wi-Fi network credentials.
  2. Enter Your Data: Fill in the required fields. For a URL, simply paste the web address. For Wi-Fi, enter the network name and password.
  3. Customise (Optional): Pick your preferred foreground and background colours to match your brand.
  4. Generate: The QR code updates automatically as you type. Pick the size you want from the size menu.
  5. Download: Save your QR code as a high-resolution PNG image ready for print or digital use.

Frequently Asked Questions

What can a QR code contain?

QR codes can store website URLs, plain text, phone numbers, email addresses, Wi-Fi credentials, geographic coordinates, and vCard contacts. This tool supports the most common types: URL, text and Wi-Fi.

How do I scan a QR code?

Open your phone camera and point it at the QR code. Most modern smartphones (both Android and iPhone) can scan QR codes directly from the default camera app without needing a separate app.

Are the QR codes high enough quality to print?

Yes. You can generate QR codes at up to 400 x 400 pixels. For large print formats, we recommend scaling up the PNG in a vector editor. The QR code pattern itself is mathematically precise and will scan correctly at any printed size.

Do QR codes expire?

QR codes generated by Fixlytools do not expire. They are simple encoded images with no expiry date or server dependency. Static QR codes (like those containing a URL) remain valid as long as the destination URL is active.

Can I use the generated QR codes commercially?

Yes. All QR codes generated by Fixlytools are free for personal and commercial use. No attribution is required.
