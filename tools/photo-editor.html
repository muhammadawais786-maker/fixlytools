How to Use This Tool

This is a simple editor for quick fixes, not a replacement for full photo software. You can brighten a photo, try a filter, turn it, and add text, stickers or a hand-drawn mark. Everything happens in your browser.

  1. Upload a Photo: Click "Select Photo" or drag a JPG, PNG or WebP picture onto the box.
  2. Adjust and Filter: Move the brightness, contrast, saturation and blur sliders, or pick a filter such as black and white or sepia.
  3. Rotate, Add Text, Stickers or Drawings: Turn or flip the photo, add text or an emoji sticker and drag it into place, or switch to the brush and draw.
  4. Download: Choose JPG or PNG and click "Download Photo". Use Undo if you change your mind. Your original file is not changed.

Key Features

  • Basic Adjustments: Brightness, contrast, saturation and blur sliders with a live preview.
  • Four Quick Filters: Black and white, sepia, warm and cool.
  • Rotate and Flip: Turn left or right and mirror the photo in either direction.
  • Text, Stickers and Brush: Add text and emoji stickers you can drag, or draw freehand in any colour.
  • Undo and Reset: Step back through your changes or reset everything in one click.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a full photo editor?

No. It is a simple tool for quick changes. It has no layers, cropping, retouching or RAW support. For detailed work, use dedicated photo software.

Does it change my original photo?

No. Your edits are saved as a new file. The original stays as it was.

What happens to my text and drawings when I rotate?

They turn with the photo so they stay on the same spot of the picture. Text stays upright.

Will the download look the same as the preview?

It should look very similar. The preview is smaller, so check the saved file, especially if you used blur or a strong filter.

Why do stickers look different on different devices?

Stickers are emoji characters drawn with the fonts on your device. Phones and computers draw them in their own style.

What about transparent PNG files?

Choose PNG to keep transparent areas. JPG has no transparency, so those areas become white.

Is my photo uploaded to a server?

No. All editing happens in your browser and the photo never leaves your device.
