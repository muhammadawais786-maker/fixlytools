How to Count Words and Characters Online

Whether you are writing a blog post, an essay, a tweet, or a resume, knowing your exact word and character count matters. Fixlytools Word Counter gives you live statistics as you type — including words, characters (with and without spaces), sentences, paragraphs, reading time, and speaking time.

  1. Paste or Type Your Text: Click inside the text area and paste your content, or start typing directly. Statistics update in real time with every keystroke.
  2. Read Your Statistics: The counters below the text area show words, characters, characters without spaces, sentences, and paragraphs instantly.
  3. Check Reading & Speaking Time: The tool estimates how long it takes an average adult to read or speak your text aloud, based on standard reading rates.
  4. Clear & Start Again: Click "Clear Text" to reset the tool and start counting a new piece of content.

Key Features

  • Live Real-Time Counting: All statistics update instantly as you type or paste — no button clicking required.
  • Key Statistics: Words, Characters (with spaces), Characters (without spaces), Sentences, Paragraphs, plus reading and speaking time.
  • Reading & Speaking Time: Estimated reading time at 200 words per minute and speaking time at 130 words per minute.
  • Runs in Your Browser: The entire tool runs inside your browser. No internet connection is needed once the page has loaded.
  • Completely Private: Your text is never sent to any server. Everything stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

How does the tool count words?

Words are counted by splitting the text on spaces and line breaks and ignoring pieces that contain no letters or digits. This works for any language, including Urdu and Hindi. Hyphenated words like "well-known" are counted as one word.

How is reading time calculated?

Reading time is estimated at 200 words per minute, which is the average silent reading speed for adults. Speaking time uses 130 words per minute, the average conversational speaking rate.

Does the tool count characters with or without spaces?

Both. The tool displays character count including spaces and character count excluding spaces in separate counters.

Is there a maximum text length?

There is no hard limit. The tool runs in your browser and can handle very large documents, though extremely large texts (over 100,000 words) may slow down depending on your device.

Can I use this for SEO meta descriptions?

Yes. Google recommends meta descriptions of under 160 characters. Paste your description into the tool to check its character count before adding it to your website.
