Understanding the EMI Formula

Equated Monthly Installment (EMI) is computed with standard mathematical amortization:

EMI = [P × R × (1+R)^N] / [(1+R)^N − 1]

Where P represents Principal amount, R is the monthly rate of interest, and N is tenure in total months.
