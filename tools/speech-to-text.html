Speech to Text

Speak into your microphone and watch your words appear as text. Works in Chrome, Edge and Safari.

Speech recognition is not available in this browser. Please use Google Chrome, Microsoft Edge or Safari on a phone or computer. Firefox does not support it.
Privacy note: to turn your voice into text, Chrome and Edge send the audio to their own speech service (Google in Chrome, Microsoft in Edge). Fixlytools never receives your voice or your text. If that is not acceptable for your content, do not use this tool.

How to Convert Speech to Text Online

This voice typing tool listens through your microphone and writes what you say. It uses the speech recognition built into your browser, so there is no software to install.

  1. Choose your language: Pick the language you will speak, for example Urdu (Pakistan) or English (United States).
  2. Press Start Listening: Allow the microphone when your browser asks. The red dot shows that it is listening.
  3. Speak clearly: Talk at a normal pace in a quiet place. Your words appear in the text box as you speak.
  4. Press Stop and edit: Fix any mistakes in the text box, then copy the text or download it as a .txt file.

Key Features

  • Many languages: Includes Urdu, Hindi, Arabic, Persian, Bengali, Turkish and the major European and Asian languages.
  • Live text as you speak: See your words appear in real time and correct them straight away.
  • Keeps listening: The tool continues after short pauses until you press Stop.
  • Copy or download: Copy to the clipboard or save the text as a .txt file.
  • No sign-up: Free, with no account and no time limit.

Frequently Asked Questions

Which browsers support speech to text?

Google Chrome and Microsoft Edge on computers and Android work best. Safari supports it on recent versions. Firefox does not support browser speech recognition.

Is my voice recorded or uploaded?

Fixlytools does not record or receive your voice. However, Chrome and Edge send the audio to their own speech services (Google or Microsoft) to turn it into text, which is how the browser feature works. Avoid using this tool for confidential material.

Does it work in Urdu?

Yes. Choose Urdu (Pakistan) and speak in Urdu. Accuracy depends on your microphone, your accent and the background noise, so always read the result and correct it.

Why does it say the microphone is blocked?

Your browser asked for microphone permission and it was denied. Click the lock icon next to the address bar, allow the microphone for this site, and press Start Listening again.

Does it need an internet connection?

Yes. In Chrome and Edge the speech is processed online, so you need to be connected for it to work.

Can it transcribe an audio or video file?

No. This tool only listens to a live microphone. It cannot read an uploaded recording.

Does it add punctuation?

Usually not. You may need to add full stops and commas yourself when you edit the text.
