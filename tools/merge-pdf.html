How to Merge PDF Files Safely

Unlike other PDF mergers that upload your confidential tax files or contracts to remote cloud servers, Fixlytools performs the merge operation locally inside your browser via WebAssembly:

  1. Select two or more PDF documents from your device.
  2. Use the up/down arrows to reorder pages as needed.
  3. Click "Merge All PDFs" to immediately download the unified file.
