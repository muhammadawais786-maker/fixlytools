How to Convert Time Between Countries

Planning a call or a meeting across countries is easier when you know the exact time difference. This converter covers more than 200 countries and territories and handles daylight saving time for you.

  1. Choose the country you start from: The tool picks your own country automatically. Change it if you want. For large countries such as the USA, Canada, Australia or Russia, also choose the region or city.
  2. Choose the country to convert to: Pick the second country (and region if asked).
  3. Set the date and time: Press Now to use the current time, or choose any date and time in the first place. Switch between 12-hour and 24-hour clocks if you prefer.
  4. Read the result: You see the converted time and date, the UTC offsets and a sentence such as "United Kingdom is 5 hours behind Pakistan".
  5. Use the shortcuts: Tap a popular pair such as Pakistan to UK time or India to USA time to fill the tool in one tap.

Frequently Asked Questions

What is the time difference between Pakistan and the UK?

Pakistan (UTC+5) is 5 hours ahead of the United Kingdom in winter, when the UK uses GMT. In summer, when the UK uses British Summer Time (UTC+1), Pakistan is 4 hours ahead. Pakistan does not use daylight saving time.

What is the time difference between Pakistan and the USA?

It depends on the US region and the season. In winter, Pakistan is 10 hours ahead of New York and 13 hours ahead of Los Angeles. In summer, when US clocks move forward, it is 9 and 12 hours. Choose the US city in the tool for the exact figure on any date.

What is the time difference between India and Pakistan?

India (UTC+5:30) is 30 minutes ahead of Pakistan (UTC+5) all year round.

Why does the difference change during the year?

Many countries, including the UK, most of Europe, the USA and Canada, move their clocks forward in spring and back in autumn. Their time difference to countries without daylight saving, such as Pakistan, India, Saudi Arabia and the UAE, therefore changes by one hour.

How do I convert my meeting time for people in other countries?

Choose your country in From, the country of the other person in To, and enter the date and time of the meeting. You can repeat this for each country.

What does UTC mean?

UTC (Coordinated Universal Time) is the world time standard. Every time zone is described as an offset from it, for example UTC+5 for Pakistan or UTC-5 for New York in winter.

Which country has half-hour or 45-minute time zones?

India and Sri Lanka use UTC+5:30, Nepal uses UTC+5:45, Iran uses UTC+3:30, Afghanistan uses UTC+4:30 and Myanmar uses UTC+6:30. The tool handles these correctly.
