Frequently Asked Questions

How much can a PDF be compressed?

The amount of compression depends largely on the content. PDFs that contain high-resolution photographs can often be reduced by 50â€“80%. PDFs with mostly text may see little or no size reduction.

Will compression affect the text in my PDF?

No. Only embedded images are compressed. Text, fonts, hyperlinks, and bookmarks remain fully intact and searchable.

Why is my compressed PDF not much smaller?

If your PDF mostly contains text or already has compressed images, there is little data to reduce further. This is normal and does not indicate an error.

Is there a maximum file size for compression?

There is no strict limit, but very large PDFs (above 100 MB) may take longer to process or require more device memory. For best results, split very large PDFs first and compress each part.

Are my PDF files sent to a server?

No. All processing happens locally in your browser using WebAssembly. Your documents are never uploaded anywhere.
