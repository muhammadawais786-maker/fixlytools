How to Split a PDF File Online

Splitting a PDF allows you to extract specific pages or sections from a large document into a new, smaller PDF. Whether you need to share only one section of a report or extract a single invoice from a bulk statement, Fixlytools PDF Splitter makes it quick and private.

  1. Upload Your PDF: Click "Choose PDF File" and select the PDF document you want to split from your device.
  2. Specify Page Range: Enter the page numbers or ranges you want to extract — for example, "1-3" for the first three pages, or "5" for a single page.
  3. Split: Click "Extract Pages" and the tool processes your PDF and extracts the specified pages.
  4. Download: Download the extracted pages as one new PDF file directly to your device.

Key Features

  • Page Range Selection: Extract a single page, a range (e.g. 1–5), or multiple ranges from any PDF.
  • Preserves PDF Quality: Pages are copied into the new file without re-compressing images, preserving text, images, and formatting.
  • Instant Processing: PDFs are processed in your browser using JavaScript — no upload wait times, no server queues.
  • Works on Any PDF: Compatible with PDFs from Word, Excel, Adobe Acrobat, scanners, and most other sources.
  • Free & Private: No account required. Your documents are never uploaded to any server.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I extract non-consecutive pages?

Yes. You can enter multiple ranges separated by commas — for example "1-3, 7, 10-12" — to extract pages from different parts of the document into a single output PDF.

Will the extracted PDF maintain original formatting?

Yes. The tool extracts pages at the binary PDF level, so all text, fonts, images, and layouts are preserved exactly as in the original.

Can I split a password-protected PDF?

No. Password-protected PDFs cannot be processed. Remove the password protection first before splitting.

Is there a page count limit?

There is no fixed page limit. The tool can handle large PDFs; performance depends on your device memory and the file size.

Is my PDF uploaded to a server?

No. All splitting is done entirely in your browser using JavaScript. Your document never leaves your device.
