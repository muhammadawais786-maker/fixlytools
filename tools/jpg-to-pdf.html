How to Convert JPG Images to PDF Online

Converting photos to PDF is ideal for creating portfolios, submitting scanned documents, or packaging multiple images into a single shareable file. Fixlytools JPG to PDF Converter handles JPG, JPEG, and PNG images and assembles them into a clean, properly formatted PDF — all in your browser.

  1. Select Your Images: Click "Choose Images" and select one or more JPG, JPEG, or PNG files from your device.
  2. Check the Order: Images become PDF pages in the order they appear in the list. Remove any image you do not want.
  3. Choose Page Layout: Select Auto (page matches each image), A4 Portrait, or A4 Landscape before converting.
  4. Convert & Download: Click "Convert Images to PDF" and your image-based PDF is ready to download in seconds.

Key Features

  • Supports JPG & PNG: Convert both JPG/JPEG and PNG images into PDF pages with a single tool.
  • Multi-Image PDF: Add multiple images and combine them all into one multi-page PDF document.
  • Page Layout Options: Choose automatic image-fitted pages, A4 portrait, or A4 landscape.
  • Preview Before Converting: See every image as a thumbnail and remove any you do not need.
  • No Uploads Needed: Everything runs in your browser via pdf-lib. Your photos stay on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Can I add multiple images to one PDF?

Yes. The tool lets you add as many images as you need and combines them into a single multi-page PDF where each image becomes one page.

What page sizes are available?

You can choose Auto (each page takes the shape of its image), A4 Portrait (210 x 297 mm) or A4 Landscape (297 x 210 mm). Images are never stretched.

Does the tool support PNG images with transparency?

Yes. PNG images are accepted. However, transparent areas are rendered with a white background in the PDF output, since the PDF format does not support transparent page backgrounds.

Is the output PDF searchable (does it contain text)?

No. The PDF contains your images as pictures, not searchable text. To create a searchable PDF from a scanned document, you would need OCR (Optical Character Recognition) software.

Are my photos uploaded anywhere?

No. The conversion uses pdf-lib entirely in your browser. Your images are never sent to any server.
