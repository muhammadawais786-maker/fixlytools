How to Calculate Your Exact Age Online

Knowing your precise age in years, months, days, hours and even seconds can be useful for official forms, medical records, legal purposes or simple curiosity. This calculator works out your exact age from your date of birth to today, or to any date you choose, and the Date Difference tab counts the time between any two dates.

  1. Enter your date of birth: Pick the day, month and year from the date field. Add your time of birth if you want the seconds figure to be as exact as possible.
  2. Choose the reference date: By default the age is worked out as of today. Change the date to find your age on a past or future day.
  3. Press Calculate Age Now: You see your age in years, months and days, your next birthday countdown, and the totals in months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds.
  4. Watch the live counter: When the reference date is today, the seconds you have lived keep counting up in real time.
  5. Need the days between two dates?: Open the Date Difference tab, pick a start and an end date, and read the difference in years, months, days and business days. You can also add or subtract days, weeks, months or years from any date.

Key Features

  • Exact age to the second: Shows age in years, months and days, plus total months, weeks, days, hours, minutes and seconds.
  • Live seconds counter: A real-time counter of the seconds you have lived, running whenever the reference date is today.
  • Date Difference calculator: Count the days between two dates, with weeks, months, hours, minutes, seconds and Monday-to-Friday business days.
  • Add or subtract days: Find the date that is 30, 90 or 100 days from now, or any number of weeks, months or years before or after a date.
  • Birthday countdown: Shows the weekday you were born on and how many days remain until your next birthday.
  • Free and private: The calculation runs in your browser. Your dates are never sent to a server.

Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact age calculated?

The tool subtracts your date of birth from the reference date (today or a date you choose) and counts the complete years, then the remaining months, then the remaining days.

How are the seconds calculated?

The seconds are the time between your birth moment and the reference moment according to your device clock. If you do not enter a time of birth, midnight at the start of your birth date is used. Because it uses your local clock, the figure can shift by an hour around daylight saving changes.

How do I find the number of days between two dates?

Open the Date Difference tab, choose a start date and an end date, and press Calculate Difference. The tool shows the total days, plus the same gap in weeks, months, hours, minutes and seconds.

Does the date difference include the last day?

Not by default. From 1 January to 2 January is 1 day. Tick "Include the end date" if you want both days counted, which makes it 2.

What are business days?

Business days are the weekdays from Monday to Friday between the two dates. Public holidays are not removed because they differ from country to country.

Can I add days to a date?

Yes. In the Date Difference tab, choose a start date, pick Add or Subtract, enter an amount and a unit (days, weeks, months or years), and the new date is shown with its weekday.

Can I calculate someone else's age?

Yes. Enter any person's date of birth. The tool calculates age for any date, not just yours.

Is the age calculation accurate for leap years?

Yes. The tool handles leap years and 29 February birthdays and counts the days of every calendar year correctly.

Can I find out how old I will be on a future date?

Yes. Change the reference date to any future date to see how old you will be then.
