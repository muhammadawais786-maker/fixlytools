Frequently Asked Questions

How is exact age calculated?

The tool subtracts your date of birth from the reference date (today or a custom date) and calculates the difference in complete years, then the remaining months, then the remaining days.

What if I do not know my exact date of birth?

Enter your estimated date of birth. The tool will calculate based on whatever date you provide. For official purposes, always use the date recorded in your legal documents.

Can I calculate someone else age?

Yes. Simply enter any person date of birth. The tool calculates age for any date, not just yours.

Is the age calculation accurate for leap years?

Yes. The tool correctly handles leap years (February 29 birthdays) and gives an accurate count of days for all calendar years.

Can I find out how old I will be on a future date?

Yes. Change the reference date to any future date to calculate how old you will be at that point.
