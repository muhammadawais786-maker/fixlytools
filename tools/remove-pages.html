How to Use This Tool

Removing pages from a PDF is useful when a document has blank pages, an old cover sheet, or sections you do not want to share. Fixlytools builds a new PDF from the pages you keep, right in your browser.

  1. Upload Your PDF: Click "Choose PDF File" or drag a PDF onto the box. The tool shows the file name and how many pages it has.
  2. Enter the Pages to Delete: Type page numbers or ranges separated by commas, for example 2, 5-7. A short summary shows what will be deleted and how many pages remain.
  3. Remove the Pages: Click "Remove Pages & Download". The new PDF is built from the pages you kept.
  4. Download the Result: Your new PDF downloads straight to your device. The original file is not changed.

Key Features

  • Pages and Ranges: Delete single pages, ranges such as 5-7, or everything from a page to the end with 9-.
  • Live Summary: See exactly which pages will go and how many will remain before you download.
  • Safe by Design: You cannot delete every page, and mistyped page numbers are reported instead of being silently ignored.
  • Deleted Means Deleted: The new file is built only from the pages you keep, so removed pages are not hidden inside it.
  • Free and Private: No account and no upload. The work happens in your browser and your file stays on your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Does this change my original PDF?

No. The tool creates a new file for download and leaves the original untouched.

Are the deleted pages really gone from the new file?

Yes. The new PDF is created from only the pages you keep, so deleted pages are not stored inside it.

Will bookmarks and links still work?

Not always. The new file is built page by page, so bookmarks and some interactive items may not be carried over. Normal text, images and layout are kept.

Can I remove pages from a password-protected PDF?

No. Protected PDFs cannot be opened by this tool. Remove the password in the app that created it, then try again.

What page numbers does the tool use?

Page numbers are counted from 1 at the first page of the file, which may differ from any numbers printed on the pages themselves.

Is my PDF uploaded anywhere?

No. The pages are processed by your browser and the file never leaves your device.
