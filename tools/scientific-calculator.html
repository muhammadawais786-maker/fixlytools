    How to Use the Scientific Calculator

    1. Tap the keys or type on your keyboard. The answer appears live; press = or Enter to keep it in History.
    2. Press SHIFT for the yellow functions (sin⁻¹, ∛, x!, nCr) and ALPHA for the letters A-F, X, Y, M.
    3. Tap DEG to switch between degrees, radians and gradians, or open SETUP for number format, fractions and complex mode.
    4. Use the tabs above the calculator for STAT, DISTR, BASE-N, EQN, MATRIX, VECTOR, TABLE, CONST and CONV.

    Key Features

    • Natural results: fractions, multiples of π and simplified roots, with an S⇔D toggle and decimal, Fix, Sci and Norm display.
    • Calculation tools: trig, hyperbolic and inverse functions, logs, powers, roots, factorial, nPr, nCr, mod, GCD, LCM, Pol/Rec, degrees-minutes-seconds and engineering notation.
    • Advanced math: Σ and Π sums, numeric integral and derivative, SOLVE, CALC with variables A-F, X, Y, M, and complex numbers.
    • Modes: STAT with 1-variable statistics and linear, quadratic, log, exponential, power and inverse regression; normal, binomial and Poisson distributions; BASE-N; equations up to degree 4; matrices; vectors; function tables.
    • Reference: 40 physical constants and unit conversion for length, area, volume, mass, temperature, speed, pressure, energy, power, time and angle.
    • Private: everything runs in your browser. Nothing you type is uploaded.

    What This Tool Can and Cannot Do

    It is a study and everyday calculator. SOLVE, the integral and the derivative are numeric approximations accurate to about 10 digits, and symbolic algebra (such as exact integrals) is not included. Values are shown to 10 digits and factorials work up to 69, like a Casio. For exams, always follow your exam board’s calculator rules.

    Frequently Asked Questions

    Which Casio functions does this calculator have?

    It follows the Casio fx-991 family: SHIFT and ALPHA layers, fractions with S-D conversion, pi and square-root results, degrees-minutes-seconds, complex numbers (CMPLX), STAT with 1-variable and 7 regression types, DISTR (normal, binomial, Poisson), BASE-N, EQN (simultaneous equations and polynomials up to degree 4), MATRIX, VECTOR, TABLE, SOLVE, CALC, integral, derivative, sum and product, Pol/Rec, GCD/LCM, 40 constants and unit conversion.

    How do I use SHIFT and ALPHA?

    Press SHIFT once, then a key, to use the small yellow label above it (for example SHIFT then sin gives sin-1). Press ALPHA once, then a key, to type the red letters A to F, X, Y and M. Each press applies to the next key only.

    How do I get fractions or exact answers?

    Math style is on by default, so 1/3 stays 1/3 and sqrt(8) shows 2 root 2. Press S-D to switch between the exact form and the decimal. Choose Line style or mixed fractions in SETUP.

    Why is sin(180) exactly 0?

    The calculator recognises exact multiples of a quarter turn and returns exact values such as 0, 1 and -1, and exact halves such as sin(30) = 1/2, instead of 1.2e-16 style rounding noise.

    Is SOLVE, the integral and the derivative exact?

    They are numeric: SOLVE uses Newton and bracketing methods, the integral uses adaptive Gauss-Kronrod and the derivative uses Ridders extrapolation. Results are accurate to about 10 digits but are approximations, like on a physical calculator.

    Does this replace the exam calculator?

    This site is for study and everyday work. Check your exam rules: most exams require a physical approved calculator, and the results here are not guaranteed to match every model in rare edge cases.
