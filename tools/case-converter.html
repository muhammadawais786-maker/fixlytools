Frequently Asked Questions

What is title case?

Title case capitalizes the main words of a heading and keeps small words such as a, an, the, of, and, in and for in lower case, unless they are the first or last word. For example "The Lord of the Rings".

What is the difference between Title Case and Capitalize Each Word?

Capitalize Each Word makes the first letter of every word a capital, including small words ("The Lord Of The Rings"). Title Case follows the headline style and leaves small words lower case.

What is sentence case?

Sentence case capitalizes only the first letter of each sentence, like normal writing. The rest of the text becomes lower case, so proper names will also be lower-cased and need checking.

What are camelCase, snake_case and kebab-case?

They are naming styles used in programming. camelCase joins words with no spaces and capitals (myVariableName), snake_case joins them with underscores (my_variable_name), and kebab-case uses hyphens (my-variable-name), often for URLs and CSS.

Does it work with Urdu, Arabic or Hindi?

Those scripts have no upper and lower case, so the case buttons leave the letters unchanged. The clean-up buttons (remove extra spaces and line breaks) work with any language.

Can I undo a change?

Yes. Press Undo to go back one step. The tool remembers your last 20 changes.
