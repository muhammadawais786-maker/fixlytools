Why are iPhone Photos in HEIC Format?

Modern Apple iPhones (iOS 11+) default to High Efficiency Image Container (HEIC). While HEIC saves space on your phone, Windows PCs, Android devices, and websites often cannot open or view them.

Fixlytools converts HEIC directly to JPG or PNG right in your browser, enabling universal compatibility with every Windows, Mac, and mobile platform.
