Frequently Asked Questions

What is a HEIC file?

HEIC stands for High Efficiency Image Container. It is the format used by Apple iPhones and iPads (iOS 11 and later) to save photos. It offers better compression than JPG — storing more detail in a smaller file. However, it is not widely supported outside Apple devices.

Why can I not open HEIC files on Windows?

Windows does not include native HEIC support by default. You can install the HEIC Image Extensions from the Microsoft Store, or simply convert HEIC files to JPG using this tool for universal compatibility.

Does converting HEIC to JPG reduce quality?

There may be a slight, usually invisible, quality reduction because JPG uses lossy compression. At high quality settings (80–95%), the difference is not noticeable to the human eye.

Can I convert multiple HEIC files at once?

Currently the tool converts one file at a time. Simply repeat the process for each photo you need to convert.

Are my photos sent to a server during conversion?

No. The conversion uses the heic2any JavaScript library running entirely in your browser. Your photos stay on your device throughout the process.
