How to Scan Documents to PDF

A phone camera is a decent scanner when the light is good. Take a picture of each page, clean it up and put all pages into one PDF in the right order.

  1. Add your pages: Press "Camera" to photograph a page, or choose images you already have. Repeat for every page.
  2. Check the crop: The tool finds the edges of the page and straightens it automatically. If it missed, press the magic-wand button on a page, choose Crop and drag the four corners onto the page. The arrows change the order, the rotate button turns a page and the bin removes it.
  3. Choose a scanner look: Open a page with the magic-wand button and pick Enhance, Lighten, No shadow, Black & white, Eco, Grayscale or Invert, then fine-tune brightness and contrast. Enhance gives white paper and dark text like a real scanner.
  4. Set page size and create the PDF: Choose A4, US Letter or fit to image, then press "Create PDF" to download your file.

Key Features

  • Camera or photos: Use the live camera, your phone camera app or pictures from your gallery or computer.
  • Scanner looks: Enhance, Lighten, No shadow, Black and white, Eco, Grayscale and Invert, with brightness and contrast sliders.
  • Auto crop and straighten: Finds the page edges, removes the background and corrects a tilted photo. Drag four corners if you want to adjust it.
  • Page sizes: A4, US Letter or a page that matches the picture, with optional margins. A4 and Letter pages switch to landscape for wide pictures.
  • Private by design: Pictures and the finished PDF never leave your device.

Frequently Asked Questions

Is this a free CamScanner alternative?

It does the same core job in your browser: take or upload a photo of a page, auto-crop and straighten it, make the paper white and the text dark, then save it as a PDF or an image. There is nothing to install, no account and no watermark. It does not include the paid app's extras such as OCR text recognition or cloud storage. Fixlytools is not affiliated with CamScanner.

Can I scan a document without an app?

Yes. Open this page on your phone, tap the camera button or choose photos from your gallery, and the scanner works inside your browser. Nothing is installed and your pictures are not uploaded.

Can I save the scan as a JPG or PNG instead of a PDF?

Yes. Choose JPG or PNG in the Save as box. You get the cropped, enhanced picture instead of a PDF, which is handy for forms and applications that ask for an image. Several pages download together as a ZIP.

How do I scan an ID card, certificate or receipt with a white background?

Photograph it on a darker surface in good light, let Auto-crop find the edges, then pick the Enhance or Black and white look. Drag the four corners if the edges are not exact. The paper becomes white and the writing stays dark.

Does this tool recognise text (OCR)?

No. Every page in the PDF is a picture, so the text cannot be selected or searched. This tool is for making a clean, shareable copy of a paper page, not for converting it to editable text.

How do I get a sharp scan?

Put the page on a flat, plain surface in even light, hold the camera straight above it and keep the whole page in view. Avoid shadows from your hand or phone. The black and white look works best on printed text.

What do the scanner looks do?

Enhance makes the paper white and the text dark while keeping colours. Magic is a stronger version with bolder text for faint or thin print. No shadow evens out the light and removes shadows. Lighten brightens the picture. Black and white turns every pixel black or white, which gives small files but loses photos, stamps and light pencil marks. Eco uses light grey text to save ink. Original leaves the picture unchanged.

How does the automatic crop work and what if it gets the page wrong?

The tool looks for the largest light page against its background, cuts it out and straightens it. It works best on a pale page on a darker surface. If the edges are wrong, press the magic-wand button on the page, choose Crop and drag the four corners onto the page corners.

Why does the camera not open?

Live camera needs a secure (https) page, a camera and your permission. If it fails, use "Camera not working? Use phone camera", which opens your phone camera app, or "Choose images" to pick pictures you already have.

Can I add HEIC photos from an iPhone?

Some browsers cannot read HEIC pictures. If an image is rejected, convert it to JPG first, for example with the HEIC to JPG tool on this site, and add it again.

Are my pictures uploaded anywhere?

No. Pictures are processed in your browser and the PDF is created on your device. Only the PDF library code is downloaded from a public library host when the page opens.
