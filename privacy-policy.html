Privacy Policy

Last Updated: October 22, 2026

1. Overview & Client-Side Privacy Guarantee

At Fixlytools (accessible from fixlytools.com), your privacy is our supreme priority. A core engineering principle of our platform is that our utilities operate 100% on the client side.

When you upload an image, PDF document, or text to compress, resize, format, or convert, the processing happens directly inside your web browser using HTML5 Canvas, WebAssembly, and JavaScript. Your files are never transmitted to, inspected by, or stored on external servers or cloud storage.

2. Information We Collect

We do not require account registration, email sign-ups, or login credentials to access any of our 15 free tools. We do not collect names, credit card details, or personal files.

3. Cookies, Analytics and Third-Party Advertising

Like most modern websites, Fixlytools may utilize standard cookies and web beacons. These are used to store visitors' preferences (such as dark/light mode toggle) and track general anonymous web traffic statistics.

Google AdSense & DoubleClick Cookie: Third-party advertising vendors, including Google, use cookies to serve ads based on a user's prior visits to this website or other websites. Google's use of advertising cookies enables it and its partners to serve ads to our users based on their visit to our sites and/or other sites on the Internet.

Users may opt out of personalized advertising by visiting Google's Ads Settings or by visiting aboutads.info.

4. GDPR and CCPA Rights

Under the General Data Protection Regulation (GDPR) and California Consumer Privacy Act (CCPA), users possess rights including the right to request deletion and data portability. Because we do not store personal profiles or files, your digital footprint remains clean and entirely within your control.

5. Contact Information

If you have questions or suggestions about our Privacy Policy, please reach out via our Contact Page or email us at support@fixlytools.com.
